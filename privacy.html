Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 26, 2026

At Lumzo ("we", "our", "us"), we value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our static web application at Lumzo.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Lumzo operates entirely client-side in your web browser. We do not require account registration or logins, nor do we collect or store your personal identity, email address, or IP address on any server.

2. Local Storage & Cookies

Lumzo utilizes your browser's local storage (localStorage) strictly to preserve your selected video layout preferences and active video wall configuration between visits. This data remains on your local device and is never transmitted to us.

3. Embedded YouTube Content

Lumzo embeds video content powered by YouTube (Google LLC) using YouTube's Privacy-Enhanced Mode (youtube-nocookie.com). Interacting with YouTube embedded players subjects you to Google's policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • YouTube Terms of Service

4. Advertising & Third-Party Cookies

We may display advertisements provided by third-party ad networks, such as Google AdSense. Third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to our website. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ad Settings or www.aboutads.info.

5. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes take effect immediately upon posting to this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via our official GitHub repository.
